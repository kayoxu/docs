| **保密条款** | **Confidentiality** |
| 1. 甲乙双方同意，对在合作过程中知悉的对方的商业秘密、技术信息及其他未公开信息（以下简称“保密信息”）负有保密义务。| 1. Each party (the "Receiving Party") shall hold in strict confidence all business secrets, technical information, and other non-public information of the other party (the "Disclosing Party") obtained during the course of this cooperation (collectively, "Confidential Information"). |
| 2. 未经对方书面同意，任何一方不得向任何第三方泄露、使用或允许他人使用该保密信息。| 2. The Receiving Party shall not, without the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party, disclose any Confidential Information to any third party, nor use it for any purpose other than for the performance of this Contract. |
| 3. 本保密义务在本合同终止后持续有效。| 3. The obligations of confidentiality herein shall survive the termination or expiration of this Contract. |
